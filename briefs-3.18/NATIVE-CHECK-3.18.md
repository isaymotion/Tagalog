## Words (shown word, then the alternatives accepted)
- shops, the till: **cashier** (kaha, counter, checkout). The writer had kaha; the reviewer chose cashier.
- shops, the person at the till: **tindera** (tindero, saleslady, kahera).
- shops, the bag: **supot** (plastic, plastik, bag).
- shops: **tindahan ng gulay / isda / karne** (gulayan, isdaan, karnehan). The dictionary lists gulayan as "vegetable garden".
- shops: **flower shop, bookstore, shoe store** in English (tindahan ng bulaklak / libro / sapatos accepted).

- botika: **eye drops** (pampatak sa mata, patak sa mata), **pamahid** (ointment, cream), **patch** (pantapal, tapal), **pulbos na gamot**.
